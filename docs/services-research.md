# Solutions catalog research

Reference reviewed on 15 October 2026: [TCT solutions](https://tctenterprise.com/solutions/). The public solutions page and its five linked service families were inspected in a browser. The initial text-fetch request returned HTTP 403; the browser successfully loaded the pages.

This research informed the breadth and organization of the Yasabe catalog. The implementation uses original descriptions and the existing Yasabe template. TCT's customer names, staffing, locations, certifications, partner status, warranties and commercial claims have not been adopted as Yasabe facts.

## Implementation boundaries

- Retain the existing page structure, CSS, responsive card grid, typography, imagery and navigation design.
- Extend the existing category filters, service groups and enquiry options rather than introducing a new template.
- Keep the existing networking, AI and managed-IT anchor IDs working.
- Carry the selected new service and scope into the email draft.
- New services show that their scope requires review. No delivery durations, compliance certifications, guaranteed business outcomes or service levels have been invented for them.
- Cloudflare configuration and deployment are outside this content update.

The shared catalog contains 33 solutions across six filters. Service delivery, commercial availability and any regulated installation requirements remain matters for the site owner to confirm before publication.
